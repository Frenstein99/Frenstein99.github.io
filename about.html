About Me

Bienvenue! I am a PhD-seeking student with a strong background in astrophysics and cosmology, driven by a deep curiosity about the Universe and a genuine love for learning. I enjoy exploring complex ideas and sharing them in a way that feels clear, meaningful, and accessible. Connecting with people and exchanging ideas across cultures is something I truly value.

I was born and raised in Bagerhat, Bangladesh, where I lived until 2015. Later, I moved to Europe and eventually settled in France, which has become an important part of who I am today. Since boyhood, I have been fascinated by fundamental physics, especially Einstein, his theories of relativity, and the vast mysteries of the cosmos. Cosmology naturally drew me in it brings together wonder, imagination, and deep scientific thinking in a way that continues to inspire me every day.

This journey would not have been possible without the kindness and support I received in France. The French government and the French people played a crucial role by providing both financial and moral support when I needed it most. After the Almighty Creator (Allah SWT), I feel a deep sense of gratitude toward France for helping shape my path. Even my name, Frênstein, reflects this connection, a personal symbol linking France and Einstein, gratitude and inspiration.

Many people ask me whether I believe in God. My answer is simple and honest: yes. I am a Muslim, and I truly believe that science and faith are not opposites, but complementary ways of understanding existence. Like Einstein, I see science as a way to explore how the Universe works, and faith as a way to reflect on why it exists.

Outside academics, I am a cheerful, talkative, and positive person. I enjoy meaningful conversations, sharing ideas, jokes, and inspirational stories. Keeping a smile helps me stay focused, grounded, and mentally balanced. I love discussing a wide range of topics, anything that is informative, educational, or simply thought-provoking. I also enjoy communicating in different languages, which helps me connect with people from diverse backgrounds.

My long-term dream is to become a cosmologist and dedicate my life to understanding the Universe. I remain committed, curious, and inspired and I hope to stay that way for the rest of my journey.

Allez… Courage.
